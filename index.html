🛟 How to Use

1

Read the source first

Read the full text without help. Mark the words that block your understanding. Then open the panels.

2

Click the theme words

Klein-blue words are the theme words of the article. Click one to open its note. Write one sentence with it.

3

Break down long sentences

The Analysis panel shows the main clause, clauses, and phrases. It also gives you a writing tip.

4

Practice and write

Do the quiz for instant feedback. Then use the discussion and writing tasks to turn input into output.

🎨 Design

Theme color · Klein blue

Theme words, navigation, and highlights use International Klein Blue (#002FA7). Lemon, coral, mint, and violet add energy without noise.

Reading comes first

The source text uses a serif font and wide line spacing. Each paragraph opens three panels. The layout adapts to phones.

Data-driven and easy to extend

Each article is one JSON file. Paste a new article to your AI agent. It creates the file and runs build.py.
